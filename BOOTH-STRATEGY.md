# NavTech at Taj-Tech: buyer demonstration strategy

Working brief · 22 September 2026 · local story prototype implemented, sales hypothesis unvalidated

## Current implementation

The [local `/demo`](http://localhost:5173/demo) implements one recruitment-availability story in RU/EN alongside the original quiz and `/screen`. A fictional application and six authored queue records connect an availability clarification to recomputed Prism counts. Original facts stay inspectable; there is no ranking, match score, production HR integration or quiz-profile write. Optional live OpenAI conversation follows the real touch state and uses paid quota only after explicit voice activation. Separate optional camera gaze runs locally after camera opt-in; it cannot start the microphone or paid voice. It uses position/presence, not identity or trait inference.

The [local `/stage`](http://localhost:5173/stage) now runs the silent 18-second four-beat story with sample labels, large text, a touchscreen invitation on every beat, pause/replay/manual controls and RU/EN. It reads the same sample rules; loop cycles are not visitor events. It does not request camera/microphone, connect to a model or replace `/screen`. This is a content preview for an agreed future shared-screen slot; actual partner allocation, display dimensions/orientation and routing remain unconfirmed.

A local sample pilot plan and anonymous tab-scoped rehearsal JSON are available. The file stays on the kiosk; visitor-phone delivery still needs a separately arranged method. Human handoff stops Navi and opens a staff discussion prompt. It does not send contact details or book anything, and its click is intent rather than a qualified next step.

Technical evidence includes fast local result rendering, actual-provider historical synthetic audio probes, voice handoff/reset, real landscape/portrait stage inspections and controlled camera/visibility lifecycle tests. These are not native-language approval, physical-camera/noisy-venue validation or sales proof. See [DEMO-CHECKPOINT.md](DEMO-CHECKPOINT.md) and [BOOTH-SCRIPT.md](BOOTH-SCRIPT.md). The second HR workflow, production product connection, personal visitor delivery and human/field outcomes remain unvalidated.

## Recommendation

Build one joined story: **HR-Agent handles the individual case. Prism shows what the organization should act on.** Navi hosts the experience; a useful work result is the payoff. Keep the technology-interest quiz available as an optional attraction, not the main pitch to organizational buyers.

Keep a short BI-only route for company and government decision-makers who name an analytics problem but have no HR need. The expert acknowledges that need and uses the already-observed [public Prism sample](https://navtech.tj/#products): period, metric and previous-period comparison. Ask which organizational decision/metric and source data matter, then apply the same consent, owner and specific-next-action qualification. This is a sample, not connected customer data; invent no sector-specific features. `/screen` can separately demonstrate actual anonymous booth aggregates, never business outcomes. Define nonbuyer by lack of relevant organizational interest, not lack of an HR use case. This is an operating-script branch, not a new app workflow; the joined HR→Prism story remains primary.

Draft Russian stage proposition for rehearsal: **«Ответ человеку. Решение руководителю.»** Identify HR-Agent × Prism alongside it. Touchscreen opener: **«Дайте ИИ HR-задачу.»** One start action, visible RU/EN choice, then a simple choice between candidate applications and employee questions. These are copy hypotheses, not proven winning messages.

## Why this direction

This is a judgment-based comparison, not an experiment or numerical forecast.

| Concept | Strength | Weakness | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current interest quiz | Easy entry, playful, supplies live booth aggregate data | Weak evidence of an HR workflow; personal profile can distract a buyer | Keep optional |
| Scripted product tour | Clear product explanation, reliable sequence | Visitor is passive; easily forgotten | Use for silent large-screen loop |
| Hands-on operational challenge + linked insight + takeaway | Relevant task, meaningful visitor control, visible product relationship | Requires a robust example, clear provenance and rehearsal | Recommended primary touchscreen experience |

The research supports design principles, not a NavTech conversion benchmark:

- Salesforce reports more than 10,000 agents built at Dreamforce 2024 from attendees’ described business use cases, with prototypes to take away. It illustrates specific work and a usable outcome. It is a vendor account at a very different staffing scale, not independent ROI evidence. [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/agentforce-launch-zone-announcement/)
- CEIR/IAEE’s research summary emphasizes hands-on and one-to-one demonstrations and access to product experts. It supports pairing the kiosk with a human conversation; the summary is not a full survey or local lead forecast. [CEIR/IAEE summary](https://www.iaee.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Cheat-Sheet-Reports-1-4.pdf)
- Duolingo’s character interaction illustrates purposeful conversation and visitor-directed topic changes. Apply this to Navi’s responsiveness, not an assumption that a consumer learning character drives B2B conversion. [Duolingo](https://blog.duolingo.com/ai-and-video-call/)
- Paradox presents concrete candidate tasks through conversation; Tableau Pulse connects metrics with explanations and guided investigation. These are useful product examples, not evidence of identical NavTech capabilities. [Paradox](https://www.paradox.ai/solutions/candidates), [Tableau Pulse](https://www.tableau.com/products/tableau-pulse)

## Three layers of the physical experience

### Walkway: understand without stopping

Use the implemented `/stage` 18-second silent-readable loop on NavTech’s agreed share of the large screen: one familiar question → one short work output → one management insight. Show the transformation itself, not a wall of promises. No contact details, visitor speech or private case content on the shared screen. Agree partner content/time allocation; do not assume exclusive use. NavTech’s banner establishes the offer; the partner’s banner remains theirs.

### Touchscreen: make one useful change

The proposed duration is about 90 seconds, subject to rehearsal:

| Time target | Visitor action | Visible proof |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | Choose candidate applications or employee questions | A familiar task with a clear expected output |
| 10–35s | Try an actual approved HR example | A concise case answer or recruiter brief, with evidence |
| 35–50s | Change one relevant fact or ask a follow-up | A meaningful correction, source, or explicit missing information |
| 50–70s | Follow the same case into Prism | One useful aggregate insight and an explanation the visitor can explore |
| 70–90s | Receive useful output; choose next action | Result first, optional team-process discussion afterward |

Hiring example: use a synthetic or expressly consented application. Show a structured recruiter brief with exact answer evidence and missing facts. Let the visitor change a task fact such as availability. Keep the human decision explicit; do not rank real people or infer personality/skills from the visitor quiz, voice or face.

Employee example: answer a question from an approved sample onboarding/FAQ source. Changing a detail should change the answer or expose a needed human handoff. Prism could show recurring question topics or unresolved requests, provided the same scenario actually supplies those data. Label sample datasets clearly until a real demo connection is established.

The public HR demo already provides recruiter-card, queue and onboarding patterns worth reusing. The public Prism demo provides period/metric/comparison exploration. The new work is the **observable connection between one case and one organizational insight**. Neither public mini demo currently proves that bridge.

Keep three data contexts distinct: an isolated HR scenario, any sample aggregate dataset, and actual anonymous booth events. Never imply that synthetic candidates are forum visitors. Preserve the existing separate live booth analytics view as “this booth runs on data.”

The useful result must be visible without opening a disclosure or waiting for voice or database saving. Supporting evidence can expand. Touch-only operation must remain useful; clearly label any offline/sample replay instead of presenting it as live model output.

### Beside the kiosk: turn interest into a relevant conversation

Staff 1 hosts near the walkway and invites/qualifies politely. Staff 2 demonstrates the product and answers business questions. Move a 3–5-minute conversation away from the active touch surface so the next visitor can participate. Keep the passage clear and let spectators understand the loop without joining a queue.

Capacity illustration, not forecast: 90 seconds interaction +20 seconds reset =110 seconds, or about 32 theoretical kiosk sessions/hour. At an assumed 50–60% utilization, that is about 16–20/hour. A five-minute expert conversation allows at most 12/hour before breaks. Handoff capacity may be the limiting factor; validate these durations onsite.

## Navi’s role

Use fast touch acknowledgment, local gaze, varied irregular idle motion, interruptible speech and one purposeful contextual remark followed by space. Personalize from chosen workflow, volunteered name/self-description, actual answers and changes. Let the visitor make the task harder and see a relevant response.

Do not infer identity, age, gender, emotion, personality or appearance from the camera; no appearance-based roasting, fake mindreading or mandatory compliments. Camera processing remains local and position-only. No camera imagery or contact fields belong in model context or aggregate analytics.

Russian and English voice should stay short and responsive; naturalness and noisy-venue performance need human rehearsal. Current OpenAI conversation is not evidence of a production Speech Kit integration. Large-screen comprehension must not depend on audio, and near-kiosk speech must not compete with the shared stage.

## Conversion after value

Primary action: **«Разобрать задачу моей команды»**. Deliver a useful one-page pilot brief/download without a mandatory contact wall. The current `/demo` provides a locally downloadable sample plan. A visitor-specific brief and phone delivery are still proposed; the kiosk file alone is not personal delivery.

The brief should contain a visitor-confirmed organizational task, relevant HR-Agent step, Prism management question/KPI, data/source prerequisites, human owner, initial scope, success measurement and assumptions. Do not fabricate savings, prices, integration readiness or commitments. Economics estimates require visitor-supplied baselines and an explicit estimate label; response latency is not business time saved.

Draft staff close:

> Где в вашей организации возникает такая задача?
>
> Давайте на короткой встрече разберём один ваш процесс и определим, что понадобится для пилота.

A **qualified conversation** has a relevant organization, self-described role/influence, a concrete workflow/problem and interest in exploring fit. A **qualified agreed next step** additionally has explicit consent, a named owner and an agreed action/date/time or agreed scheduling process. A badge scan/contact alone qualifies for neither. “Request saved” does not mean “meeting booked.”

Proposed staff practice: reconcile notes and ownership at each day’s end; follow up within one business day with a personal summary, agreed next action and useful artifact. This is an operational target, not outreach already sent. Confirm actual calendar availability before booking. No automatic outreach is authorized by this document.
