# NavTech booth: host and expert run sheet

22 September 2026 · local sample demonstration · intended duration 60–90 seconds, to be rehearsed

Use `/demo` for this recruitment story. The five-question game at `/` is a separate alternative. This script does not assert a production HR-Agent → Prism integration or measured business savings.

## Two people, one clear handoff

**Host:** stands beside the walkway, offers one short invitation, helps only when needed, controls the queue and resets the touchscreen. Do not block the aisle or talk over the visitor/Navi. **Expert:** stays beside, rather than in front of, the active touch surface; takes the organizational conversation and agrees any next action. If the expert is occupied, offer the silent demonstration or an agreed return time; never imply a meeting is booked.

Exact Russian invitation:

> «Есть минутка? Измените один ответ в отклике — и увидите, где команда теряет время на уточнения.»

If interested: «Это учебный пример из шести откликов. Можно пройти касаниями или поговорить с Нави.» Do not automatically enable the microphone for a passer-by. Let the person choose RU/EN and tap **«Поговорить»** themselves. The microphone sends audio to OpenAI; the app does not save visitor audio/transcripts. `/demo` has pointer/touch gaze and optional local camera gaze, enabled separately in «Настройки камеры». Camera presence gives only a bounded quiet wave, never a microphone start. Keep volume local to the kiosk and within organizer limits.

## Demonstration beats

| Target time | Visitor and screen | Host words, only if needed |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10s | «Разобраться за минуту» opens HR-04 | «Один отклик. Пока непонятно, когда человек готов к интервью.» |
| 10–25s | Read original role, experience and vague availability; choose «На этой неделе» | «Уточним доступность. Как меняется следующий шаг рекрутера?» |
| 25–40s | HR brief says «Согласовать время»; original source remains inspectable | «Теперь можно предложить время. Само интервью мы ещё не назначали.» |
| 40–55s | Prism shows availability clarification **3 → 2 из 6** | «Это очередь работы команды: у двух откликов ещё нужно уточнить доступность.» |
| 55–70s | Change to «Пока не уточнили» or «В следующем месяце»; inspect queue if curious | «Попробуйте другой ответ. Следующий шаг и очередь изменятся вместе.» |
| 70–90s | Optional «Разобрать задачу моей команды» or sample plan | «Есть похожий этап у вас? Можем разобрать один процесс с коллегой.» |

Metric wording matters: **«ждут уточнения доступности»** means the `clarify` stage: three initially, two after week/month. **«Интервью ещё не назначено»** means five of six in every variant. Knowing a week is not the same as booking an appointment. With «Пока не уточнили», no uncertainty has been removed. Avoid saying that every click improves the result.

Navi can answer, explain the latest choice, entertain a brief visitor-led challenge and then listen. Touch alone changes this scenario. A spoken hypothetical is not an actual input change; Navi should name the button to tap. Do not describe this as an assessment of the visitor or a real applicant decision. An answer change cancels outdated narration; the result itself never waits for voice.

## Conversion conversation, after value

Tapping **«Разобрать задачу моей команды»** ends Navi's microphone/playback and clears its caption so the expert can talk. Closing that panel does not restart voice. An explicit later tap on «Поговорить» starts a fresh conversation.

Ask these two qualification questions in conversation, not as a mandatory form:

1. «В какой организации вы работаете и как участвуете в решениях по этому процессу?»
2. «На каком конкретном этапе сейчас возникает задержка и как вы понимаете, что она устранена?»

Relevant buyer close:

> «Предлагаю коротко разобрать один ваш процесс и определить данные и критерий пилота. С вашего согласия запишем контакт, ответственного с нашей стороны и договоримся о следующем шаге. Как вам удобнее согласовать время?»

Only record a qualified agreed next step when organization, role/influence, concrete task, interest, consent, owner and a specific action/date or scheduling agreement are established. A button tap, contact or promised discussion is not that outcome. Keep consented staff notes separate from public screens and anonymous rehearsal events. Confirm actual availability before saying “booked.” This document does not send messages.

**BI-only organizational route:** A company or government decision-maker may have a valid Prism need without an HR use case. If they say hiring is irrelevant and name a BI problem: «Понял, найм сейчас не ваша задача. Давайте посмотрим, какое решение вам нужно принимать по данным». The expert opens the [public Prism sample](https://navtech.tj/#products), shows its observed period, metric and previous-period comparison controls, and asks: «Какое решение или показатель важен вашей организации и из каких источников берутся данные?» Clearly call it a sample dashboard; do not imply connected customer data or sector-specific capabilities. Use the same relevance, consent, named owner and specific-next-action qualification above. Optional `/screen` shows actual anonymous booth session aggregates as a separate proof, not business outcomes. Keep HR→Prism as the primary joined story.

“Nonbuyer” means no relevant organizational interest in either HR or BI, not merely no recruitment need.

Friendly nonbuyer close:

> «Спасибо, что попробовали. Если такая задача появится у вашей команды, подходите — покажем на конкретном процессе. Хорошего форума!»

**Download truth:** «Скачать пример плана» saves a labelled sample Markdown file to this kiosk. It does not send anything to the visitor's phone, email or messenger. A staff member must separately arrange a copy if requested. Do not tell visitors to scan a nonexistent QR code. The sample owner/scope are placeholders, not an agreed personalized pilot.

### Stage operator steps

1. Open `/stage` in the agreed display slot. It runs 4s question → 5s clarification → 5s Prism insight → 4s touchscreen invitation. The six records are always sample; availability clarification changes 3→2/6, while five interviews remain unbooked.
2. Open **«Управление»** for pause/resume, **«Сначала»**, language and **«Ручной показ · без анимации»**. The four small beat buttons inspect a frame and pause the loop; keyboard Enter/Space works with focused controls. Language changes also pause. Replay starts from the question unless manual/reduced-motion mode is active.
3. Reduced-motion preference opens a useful static Prism result with controls; select beats manually. Hiding the document pauses it and does not resume automatically on return. Press «Продолжить» when ready. The browser’s hidden-pane setting is not necessarily a hidden document; verify actual behavior on the booth browser.
4. No mic/camera, sound, paid model session or loop-cycle analytics exist on `/stage`. Do not substitute it for actual booth aggregates or call its numbers live. Do not mirror visitor captions, faces or contacts to the display.

## Before opening / between visitors

- Run locally, open `/demo` on the touchscreen. Confirm the loopback voice relay and internet if voice is offered. The touch scenario remains local after assets load; voice requires OpenAI connectivity/quota. Do not promise offline persistence.
- Camera is **off by default**. Open «Настройки камеры» → «Включить камеру» and grant the browser/OS permission on the actual kiosk. Wait for detector readiness, then close settings. No face image is shown or sent elsewhere. Check left/right gaze and lighting with a consenting operator. Touch/pointer takes priority for 2.2 seconds; reduced motion disables gaze/movement.
- The visible «Камера включена» control turns capture off directly; settings also provide a stop button and status. Permission denial/missing camera preserves touch and independent voice. Cancel a pending permission request if needed; a late stream is released rather than activated.
- Camera can remain operator-enabled between visitors; «Начать заново» clears gaze/arrival state and invalidates the previous detector epoch. Staff handoff pauses gaze/arrival and leaves Navi neutral. Leaving/hiding the page stops video tracks; it never silently resumes capture. Re-enable explicitly after return. Closing settings alone does not turn an already enabled camera off.
- Check sound volume and echo on actual hardware; review Russian and English with fluent speakers. Use the technical audition files as inputs for review, not an approval of language quality.
- If voice is slow/unavailable, tap «Остановить» and continue by touch immediately. There is no automatic paid reconnect, system-voice fallback or need to restart the entire interaction.
- For live visitors choose **«Посетитель»** under «Репетиция» before the run. Staff and automated runs stay marked as tests. All six cases remain sample regardless of event origin.
- At handoff verify the button returns to «Поговорить» and Navi is quiet. After the conversation tap **«Начать заново»**: result, choice, caption and voice reset. Do not hand a new visitor the previous session. Language may then be changed; restarting voice always requires a new explicit tap.
- Export the tab-local journal before reload/closing. It is capped at 500 events and has no durable store. The plan download is separate. Never count the six fictional cases as visitors or the handoff click as a qualified lead.
